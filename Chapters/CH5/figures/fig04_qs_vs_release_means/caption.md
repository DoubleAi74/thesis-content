Quasi-stationary and release means across internal death rates at
$\lambda=1$ and $\delta=0.05$. The quasi-stationary mean
$\langle X\rangle_{\rm QS}=a/(a-1)$ is identically the conditional burst mean
$V_\infty/(1-b)$. At $\mu=0$, $b=0$ and both also equal $V_\infty$; once
$\mu>0$, internally cleared cells contribute zero release and place
$V_\infty$ strictly below the other two; the arrow marks that gap,
$b\langle X\rangle_{\rm QS}=3.25$, at the working point. In the $\mu=0$ case the conditional
mean and the cumulative release share a limit,
$\lim_t J(t)/I(t)=\lim_t V(t)=1+\lambda/\delta$, which diverges as $\delta$
shrinks.
