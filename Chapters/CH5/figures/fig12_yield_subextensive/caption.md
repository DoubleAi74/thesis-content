As typeset in `sections/09_several_founders.tex`, label `dist:fig:subextensive`.
This file is a copy for reference; the chapter is the source of truth.

Unconditional mean yield $V_\infty^{(k)}$ against founding
multiplicity $k$, compared with the naive independent-cell line $kV_\infty$,
for $(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$. The true yield is sub-extensive
because all founders share one cell and hence one catastrophe; for $k=2$,
$V_\infty^{(2)}=17.43$ rather than $2V_\infty=27.97$.
