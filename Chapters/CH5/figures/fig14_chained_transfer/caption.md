Chained immediate transfer with $(\lambda,\delta)=(1,1)$ and
$s=\rho=1/2$. (a) Simulated rupture-size probabilities (bars) from $10^6$
chain realisations with seed 42, with the exact negative-binomial laws
\cref{dist:eq:rklaw} (curves) for $k=1,\ldots,4$. (b) The analytic mean
interval $\E{T(k)}$ of \cref{dist:eq:meant} at fixed founding load $k$, and
the simulated mean interval at chain position $k$; both decrease over
$k=1,\ldots,6$, the latter faster because its founding loads are random and
growing.
