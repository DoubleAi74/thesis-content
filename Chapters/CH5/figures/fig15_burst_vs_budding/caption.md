Release-count laws for the BDC process at
$(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$ and a budding comparator whose mean
$p/d_{\Icell}$ is matched to the BDC conditional mean
$V_\infty/(1-b)=17.23$. The BDC law conditioned on bursting is geometric on
$\{1,2,\ldots\}$, whereas the budding law is geometric on $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$
and retains probability $0.055$ of zero release. With the means matched the
two laws are close everywhere else; the difference that survives is the atom
at zero, which no choice of $p$ and $d_{\Icell}$ can remove.
