# Figure: AK contrast (paper caption)

Conceptual contrast between the Antal--Krapivsky terminal-population generating function and the no-catastrophe probabilities studied here.
The underlying two-type one-way branching skeleton---birth, death, and irreversible conversion---is shared (bottom).
The left readout tracks the joint law of the terminal counts through $\mathcal A(x,y,t)=\mathbb{E}[x^{X_t}y^{Y_t}]$ with generating-function initial data $\mathcal A(x,y,0)=x$ and $\mathcal B(x,y,0)=y$, and without a global absorbing catastrophe.
The right readout asks whether a type-specific catastrophe rate of total intensity $\delta_1x+\delta_2y$ has yet fired, yielding $S(t)=\Prob_{(1,0)}(\tau_{\!c}>t)$ and $G(t)=\Prob_{(0,1)}(\tau_{\!c}>t)$ with survival initial data $S(0)=G(0)=1$.
Setting $\delta_1=\delta_2=0$ while keeping those survival data produces only the trivial solution $S\equiv G\equiv1$; recovering the earlier backward system requires restoring the generating-function initial data $x,y$ (equation-level map in \cref{app:AK-recovery}).
Symbolic schematic only.
