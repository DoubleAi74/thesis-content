# Figure: genealogy tree (paper caption)

One realisation of the two-type continuous-time genealogy with catastrophe.
The process starts from one type-1 individual at $t=0$.
Filled nodes mark births; blue-ringed vermillion nodes mark irreversible type~$1\to2$ conversion; crosses mark ordinary death; filled red discs mark lineages terminated by the global catastrophe.
Horizontal position is physical time; vertical placement is a leaf-order layout for legibility.
The dashed red vertical line is the catastrophe wavefront: every lineage still alive at that instant ends simultaneously and the process is absorbed in $(\mathsf R,\mathsf R)$.
This realisation contains 51 births, 9 conversions, 27 ordinary deaths, and 25 lineages killed by catastrophe at $t\approx5.75$.
Parameters: $\lambda_1=1.0$, $\mu_1=0.55$, $\nu=0.40$, $\lambda_2=0.90$, $\mu_2=0.50$, $\delta_1=0.05$, $\delta_2=0.08$.
Exact continuous-time simulation (Gillespie) with seed 2869 up to horizon $T=9$.
