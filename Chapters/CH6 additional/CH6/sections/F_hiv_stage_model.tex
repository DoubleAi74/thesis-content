\section{The HIV stage model}
\label{p:app:hivstages}

\Cref{p:sec:hiv} argues that HIV is not a birth--death--catastrophe process
but that the skeleton \cref{p:eq:skeleton} still carries it, on kernels
$(S_{\rm HIV},g_{\rm HIV})$ supplied by an eclipse-structured
short-productive-pulse model. This appendix records that model explicitly.
It is a candidate, not a fitted result: the point is to fix which system is
meant by \cref{p:fig:hiv-stages}.

Write the system as follows. Let
$L_0$ be a stimulated latently infected cell; two eclipse pathways run in
parallel, $A$ of high release potential and multi-stage, $B$ low and brief:
\begin{equation}
L_0\xrightarrow{\alpha_A}E_1^{(A)},
\quad
L_0\xrightarrow{\alpha_B}E^{(B)},
\quad
E_j^{(A)}\xrightarrow{\ \omega\ }E_{j+1}^{(A)},
\quad
E_n^{(A)}\xrightarrow{\ \omega\ }I^{(A)},
\quad
E^{(B)}\xrightarrow{\alpha_B}I^{(B)}.
\label{p:eq:hiv-paths}
\end{equation}
Eclipse cells divide or die without releasing virus,
\begin{equation}
E\xrightarrow{\rho_{\rm div}}E+E,
\qquad
E\xrightarrow{\mu_E}\varnothing,
\label{p:eq:hiv-eclipse-events}
\end{equation}
with $\rho_{\rm div}\approx\mu_E\approx0.5/$day; productive cells release at
constant rate and die,
\begin{equation}
I^{(A)}\xrightarrow{p_A}I^{(A)}+\Vfree,
\quad
I^{(A)}\xrightarrow{\delta_I}\varnothing,
\quad
I^{(B)}\xrightarrow{p_B}I^{(B)}+\Vfree,
\quad
I^{(B)}\xrightarrow{\delta_I}\varnothing,
\label{p:eq:hiv-prod-events}
\end{equation}
with $p_A\gg p_B$ matching the bimodal release distribution and free virus
clearing at rate $c$. Suppressing the dual path, the stage equations are
\begin{equation}
\ddt{E_j}=\omega E_{j-1}+\rho_{\rm div}E_j-(\mu_E+\omega)E_j,
\quad j=1,\dots,n,
\qquad
\ddt{I^{(A)}}=\omega E_n-\delta_I I^{(A)},
\label{p:eq:hiv-stage-odes}
\end{equation}
with $E_0\equiv0$, the initial cohort entering as $E_1(0)=\alpha_AL_0$, and
$\Vfree'=p_AI^{(A)}-c\Vfree\ (+\,p_BI^{(B)})$. Division in eclipse is a net
gain of $+\rho_{\rm div}E_j$ and appears once, not on both sides.
