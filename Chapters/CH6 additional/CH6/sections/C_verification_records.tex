\section{Verification record}
\label{p:app:verification}

Every quantitative claim of this chapter was checked analytically where
possible and numerically where not. The suite tests
\crefrange{p:eq:Icell}{p:eq:Vfree} at four levels: the integrity of the
kernels; the reduction to single-cell formulae without reinfection; the
structural limits available in closed form; and an independent stochastic
simulation of a finite cohort. The parameter sets are
$(\lambda,\mu,\delta)\in\{(1,0,0.1),\,(1,0.2,0.05),\,(1,0,0.5),\,
(1,0.9,0.1),\,(1,0.5,1/3)\}$ --- the three regimes of \cref{p:tab:trio} and
two further sets chosen to spread the kernel shapes. Fifty-four checks, all
passing (\cref{p:tab:verification}).

Three checks are shown rather than tallied, because what they establish is
used in the body: \cref{p:fig:D-exp} is test~D, that the construction
contains the classical model exactly rather than approximately, and
\cref{p:fig:EF} is tests~E and~F, the growth rate and the threshold.

The reinfection case ($\gamma T>0$) is checked indirectly, through the
characteristic equation --- tests C, E, F and G --- which any correct linear
theory must satisfy; a direct multi-generation stochastic check is left to
future work.

source (Python~3.14, NumPy~2.4, Matplotlib~3.10). From the chapter
directory:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 verification/verify_result_20_1.py
# writes verify_result_20_1_report.txt, metrics, verify_figures/*.pdf
\end{verbatim}
\noindent
Run under Python~3.14.6, NumPy~2.4.2 and Matplotlib~3.10.8; the suite has no
other dependency.
