\section{Figure and simulation record}
\label{var:app:figures}

Line plots in this chapter are vector PDFs, drawn in the house palette of
\cref{var:sec:introduction}'s sibling chapters, from either a closed form or
a recorded-seed simulation of the equations as printed. Two schematics remain
inline TikZ; two more are standalone TikZ packages. The spatial automaton is
the one remaining raster, cropped from the original frames.
The rise--run--ruin--rejuvenation panels
\cref{var:fig:vbc-two,var:fig:vbc-many} were re-simulated at the invented
tuple $(\phi,c,\lambda,\delta,\omega)=(4,0.8,0.5,0.25,0.15)$, because the
parameters of the original rasters were never recorded: the cycle is the
same, the amplitudes are not.
