\paragraph{The $\mu=0$ specialisation.} $b=0$, $a=1+\delta/\lambda$,
$D\equiv0$, $\Ifix=I$,
$V_\infty=1+\lambda/\delta=\langle X\rangle_{\rm QS}$,
$\E{T_{\rm prod}}=\lambda^{-1}\log(1+\lambda/\delta)$,
$V_\infty^{(k)}=k+\lambda/\delta$, and
$\pr{\Kb=k}=s\rho^{k-1}$ with $s=\delta/(\lambda+\delta)$, $\rho=1-s$: the
burst size is geometric on $\{1,2,\ldots\}$ with success probability $s$, and
every realisation bursts. Several coincidences hold only here ---
$V_\infty=\E{\Kb\mid\text{burst}}$, and
$\E{\tau\mid\text{burst}}=\E{T_{\rm prod}}$ --- and none of them survives
$\mu>0$.
