# Figure 5.8 — caption

As typeset in `sections/06_burst_size.tex`, label `dist:fig:jointtauk`.
This file is a copy for reference; the chapter is the source of truth.

Joint density, with respect to time and counting measure, of burst
time $\tau$ and burst size $\Kb$ for $(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0,0.1)$. The
overlay is the conditional mean $\E{\Kb\mid\tau=t}=K(t)/J(t)$, rising from
$1$ towards $1+2\lambda/\delta=21$; the upward tilt of the mass is
size-biasing made visible.
