The size of a late burst at $(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$. The
conditional mean $\E{\Kb\mid\tau=t}=K(t)/J(t)$ of \cref{dist:eq:sizebias}
rises from $1$ at founding to $(a+1)/(a-1)=33.46$; the quasi-stationary mean
$a/(a-1)=17.23$ is marked for contrast, their ratio being $(a+1)/a=1.94$.
