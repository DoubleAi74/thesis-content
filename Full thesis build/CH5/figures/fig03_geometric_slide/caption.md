State probabilities $p_n(t)$ at successive times for
$(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$. On the logarithmic vertical scale each
geometric law is a straight line whose ratio $P(t)$ increases towards $1/a$;
the inset shows that approach directly. The curves fall in $n$ and rise in
$t$: the load spreads as $P$ slides.
