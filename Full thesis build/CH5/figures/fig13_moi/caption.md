Multiplicity of infection at $(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$.
(a) Release fluxes $g_k(t)=\delta K_k(t)$ from \cref{dist:eq:gkflux} for
$k=1,\ldots,4$: early ages are ordered superlinearly in founding load, while
late fluxes reverse as multi-founder lineages fix faster, so the enhancement
is age-dependent rather than a pointwise ordering valid for all $t$.
(b) Conditional mean burst $V_\infty^{(k)}/(1-b^k)$ increases with $k$; the
dashed line is the $k=1$ value $a/(a-1)=17.23$.
