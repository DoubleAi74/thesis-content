# Figure: process schematic (paper caption)

The two-type birth--death--conversion process with a single global absorbing catastrophe.
A type-1 individual (blue) gives birth at per-capita rate $\lambda_1$, dies at rate $\mu_1$, and converts irreversibly into type~2 (vermillion) at rate $\nu$; a type-2 individual gives birth at rate $\lambda_2$ and dies at rate $\mu_2$, with no route back to type~1.
Ordinary death changes the population counts by one individual while the process continues.
Catastrophe is a shared absorbing event: in state $(x,y)$ it fires at total rate $\delta_1x+\delta_2y$ and sends the process to $(\mathsf R,\mathsf R)$.
The probabilities $S(t)$ and $G(t)$ are the probabilities that this clock has not fired by time $t$, starting from one type-1 or one type-2 individual respectively.
Symbolic schematic only; rates as in \cref{sec:model}.
