How it works:
The Koenigs function linearizes the dynamics of the logistic map near the fixed point 0.

Instead of computing large divisions, we calculate the product form:
Ψ(z) = z · Π (1 - f⁽ⁿ⁾(z))
Colors represent the phase of Ψ(z), and dark lines represent the grid of the linearized coordinates.
