\section{Standing setup}
\label{a:sec:setup}

Throughout, $(Z_n)_{n\ge0}$ denotes the binary Galton--Watson process with offspring
probability generating function
\begin{equation}
\label{a:eq:pgfbinary}
\phi(z) = p z^2 + (1-p),
\qquad p\in[0,\tfrac12),
\end{equation}
started from $Z_0=1$. The mean population is $\ex{Z_n}=(2p)^n$. Writing
$S_n=\pr{Z_n>0}$ for the survival probability, the first-step relation
$S_{n+1}=1-\phi(1-S_n)$ specialises to the logistic recursion
\begin{equation}
\label{a:eq:SnIter}
S_{n+1} = 2p\,S_n - p S_n^2,
\qquad S_0=1.
\end{equation}
Extinction is certain for $p\le\tfrac12$ (\ChM). For $p<\tfrac12$ the late-time
survival probability admits the asymptotic
\begin{equation}
\label{a:eq:lateS}
S_n \sim A(p)\,(2p)^n,
\qquad
A(p) = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{S_n}{(2p)^n},
\end{equation}
and the conditional mean converges to the reciprocal of this constant:
\begin{equation}
\label{a:eq:qsmean}
\ex{Z_n\mid Z_n>0} = \frac{(2p)^n}{S_n}
\;\xrightarrow{n\to\infty}\;
\frac{1}{A(p)} .
\end{equation}
Existence of the limit $A(p)\in(0,\tfrac12]$ is proved in \cref{a:sec:product} via the
infinite product. The full derivation of the conditional variance and the Yaglom limit
is in \ChM; here only \cref{a:eq:Apdef,a:eq:qsmean} are needed.

We adopt the standing convention $A(0):=\lim_{p\downarrow0}A(p)=\tfrac12$ when endpoint
statements require it. The continuous-time comparison constant
\begin{equation}
\label{a:eq:Acts}
\Ac(p) = \frac{1-2p}{1-p}
\end{equation}
was derived in \ChM from the linear birth--death process with
$p=\lambda/(\lambda+\mu)$; that derivation is not repeated. Write
$\varepsilon=1-2p$ for the distance from criticality and $r=2p$ for the logistic
multiplier.
