# Figure: derivation pipeline (paper caption)

Roadmap of the exact solution (schematic of \cref{sec:main_result}).
The vermillion stage is the type-2 input of \cref{sec:autonomous}: the autonomous solution supplies $r_{2,-}$ and the moving coordinate $z_2$.
Blue stages are the type-1 pipeline: substitute $\widehat G$ into the type-1 Riccati equation; shift by the long-time root $h$; linearise by $X=-Z'/Z$; change independent variable to $z_2$; recognise the Gauss hypergeometric equation with basis $U,V$; impose $\widehat S(0)=1$ by the Wronskian coefficients $K_U,K_V$; reverse the changes of variable to obtain the boxed formula of \cref{thm:main}.
The special function is not an ansatz: it appears because the type-2 probability contributes a single moving rational pole.
