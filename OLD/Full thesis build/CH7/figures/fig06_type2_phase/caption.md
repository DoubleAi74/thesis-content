# Figure: type-2 phase (paper caption)

Geometry of the autonomous type-2 probability.
(a)~Right-hand side $f(G)=\lambda_2G^2-(\lambda_2+\mu_2+\delta_2)G+\mu_2=\lambda_2(G-r_{2,-})(G-r_{2,+})$ against $G$, with equilibria $r_{2,\pm}$.
Because $f(0)=\mu_2>0$ and $f(1)=-\delta_2<0$ with positive leading coefficient, the roots straddle the unit point: $0\le r_{2,-}<1<r_{2,+}$.
The admissible window $[0,1]$ is shaded; only $r_{2,-}$ can be a long-time probability.
(b)~Phase line of $\dot G=f(G)$: $r_{2,-}$ is attracting and $r_{2,+}$ is repelling; $G(0)=1$ is drawn into $r_{2,-}$.
(c)~Relaxation of $G(t)$ from $1$ toward $r_{2,-}$.
Parameters: $\lambda_2=0.9$, $\mu_2=0.35$, $\delta_2=0.20$ (physical time in panel~(c)).
