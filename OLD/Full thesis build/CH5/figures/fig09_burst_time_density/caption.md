Defective burst-time density $\varphi(t)=\delta J(t)$ and its
conditional counterpart $\varphi(t)/(1-b)$. For
$(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$ the finite-time density has total mass
$1-b$ and the remaining mass $b=0.1884$ lies at $\tau=\infty$; for
$(1,0,0.1)$, $b=0$ and the two densities coincide. The gap between the curves
is the whole content of the defect.
