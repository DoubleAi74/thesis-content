One endpoint, three readings. The ratio $P(t)$ of
\cref{dist:thm:geometric} slides from $0$ at founding to $1/a$, and each of
the next three sections applies a different operation to that endpoint:
\cref{dist:sec:qsd} takes it as the quasi-stationary ratio,
\cref{dist:sec:burst-size} integrates the rupture hazard against it, and
\cref{dist:sec:burst-time} conditions the rupture time on the size it
produces. The first two return the same law, differing only by the
eventual-burst probability $1-b$; that coincidence is
\cref{dist:thm:identity}.
