As typeset in `sections/02_recap.tex`, label `dist:fig:joint`.
This file is a copy for reference; the chapter is the source of truth.

A representative path of the joint process $(\Xt,\Wt)$. The
intracellular load follows a birth--death path until rupture at time $\tau$,
when $\Xt$ enters the absorbing state $H$. At the same instant the released
count jumps from zero to the pre-rupture load $X_{\tau^-}$ and remains there.
The two axes share a time origin: nothing leaves the cell before $\tau$, and
everything leaves at $\tau$.
