\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

\begin{chapterabstract}
\noindent
This chapter introduces the biological and mathematical setting of the thesis.
It motivates a stochastic treatment of early \textit{Yersinia pestis}
infection, reviews the branching-process ideas used throughout and the
birth--death--catastrophe process built from them, sets out the biphasic
pathogenesis of plague and the reasons it is thought to be forced, and situates
the basic model of viral replication (BMVR) alongside the
bursting-versus-budding question that runs through the middle of the thesis.
Four research questions and a roadmap of the remaining chapters are given at
the end.
\end{chapterabstract}
