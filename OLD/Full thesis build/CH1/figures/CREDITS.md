# Chapter 1 figure credits

Images not made by the author are listed below with their source and licence.
Sourced images have been resized for print, and where noted cropped; no other
alteration has been made.

## fig_triumph/  (Figure 1.1)

**bruegel_triumph.jpg** — *The Triumph of Death*, Pieter Bruegel the Elder,
c. 1562, oil on panel, Museo del Prado, Madrid.
Commons: File:The Triumph of Death by Pieter Bruegel the Elder.jpg
Licence: **Public domain** (artist died 1569).

## fig_plague_naples/  (Figure 1.2)

**spadaro_naples_1656.png** — *Piazza Mercatello a Napoli durante la peste del
1656*, Domenico Gargiulo, called Micco Spadaro, Museo Nazionale di San
Martino, Naples. Supplied by the author as a monochrome reproduction.
Licence: **Public domain** (artist died 1675).
Note: a colour reproduction of the same painting is available at 1200x851 as
Commons File:Piazza Mercatello durante la peste del 1656 - Spadaro.jpg, also
public domain, if a colour plate is preferred.

## fig_yp_micrographs/  (Figure 1.3)

**yp_sem_flea.jpg** — Scanning electron micrograph of *Yersinia pestis* in the
foregut of a flea vector. Cropped to 4:3 and downsampled.
Source: National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID).
Commons: File:Yersinia pestis scanned with electron micrograph.jpg
Licence: **Public domain** (work of the US federal government).

**yp_wayson_hi.png** — Wayson stain of *Y. pestis*, showing the characteristic
bipolar "safety pin" appearance among leucocytes. Supplied by the author.
Source: US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.
Licence: **Public domain** (work of the US federal government).

**yp_dfa_field.jpg** — *Y. pestis*, direct fluorescent antibody stain, wide
field. Supplied by the author.
Source: CDC Public Health Image Library.
Licence: **Public domain** (work of the US federal government).
