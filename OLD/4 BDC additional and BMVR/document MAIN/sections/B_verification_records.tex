\section{Verification records}
\label{app:verification}

Every result in this chapter was checked analytically where possible and
numerically where not. This appendix records the two numerical suites,
their tallies as of the last run (2026-08-07), and how to reproduce them.

\subsection{Renewal BMVR suite (Result~\ref{res:new-vde})}

The suite \texttt{verify\_result\_20\_1.py} tests the renewal system
\eqref{eq:Icell}--\eqref{eq:Vfree} at four levels: the integrity of the
kernels; the reduction to single-cell formulae without reinfection; the
structural limits that can be checked in closed form; and an independent
stochastic simulation of a finite cohort. Parameter sets:
$(\beta,\mu,\delta)\in\{(1,0,0.1),\,(1,0.2,0.05),\,(1,0,0.5),\,
(1,0.9,0.1),\,(1,0.5,1/3)\}$.

\begin{center}

\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}clp{0.44\textwidth}c@{}}
\toprule

\midrule

  $V'=g$, $\int g=V_\infty$, $\int\Ihat=\mean{T_{\rm prod}}$, $I'=-\delta J$ &
  5/5 \\
B & $\gamma T=0$ cohort &
  $\Icell=\Icell_0\Ihat$; $\Vfree'=\Icell_0 g-c\Vfree$ vs exact
  convolution; $c=0\Rightarrow\Vfree=\Icell_0 V(t)$ &

(Earlier summaries of this suite quote 53 checks; the present version adds
one well-posedness check.) One honest gap: a full stochastic
multi-generation Gillespie check with reinfection ($\gamma T>0$) is not yet
coded. The reinfection case is already constrained by tests C, E, F, G
through the characteristic equation, which any correct linear theory must
satisfy, and agreement is expected by linearity.

\subsection{Chained immediate-transfer suite (\S\ref{sec:chained})}

exactly (the event-type decomposition of \S\ref{sec:key-observation} makes
simulation direct) and checks every quantitative claim of
\S\ref{sec:chained} across three parameter sets,
$(\beta,\delta)\in\{(1,1),\,(1,0.1),\,(2,0.5)\}$.
