\section{Verification record: renewal BMVR}
\label{app:verification}

Every quantitative claim of this chapter was checked analytically where
possible and numerically where not. This appendix records the numerical
suite for the renewal system, its tally, and how to reproduce it.

The suite \texttt{verify\_result\_20\_1.py} tests the renewal system
\eqref{eq:Icell}--\eqref{eq:Vfree} at four levels: the integrity of the
kernels; the reduction to single-cell formulae without reinfection; the
structural limits that can be checked in closed form; and an independent
stochastic simulation of a finite cohort. Parameter sets:
$(\lambda,\mu,\delta)\in\{(1,0,0.1),\,(1,0.2,0.05),\,(1,0,0.5),\,
(1,0.9,0.1),\,(1,0.5,1/3)\}$. Fifty-four checks, all passing.

One honest gap: a full stochastic multi-generation Gillespie check with
reinfection ($\gamma T>0$) is not yet coded. The reinfection case is
already constrained by tests C, E, F, G through the characteristic
equation, which any correct linear theory must satisfy, and agreement is
expected by linearity.
