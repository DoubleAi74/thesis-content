# Figure: biological reading of the two-type intracellular model (paper caption)

Biological reading of the two-type intracellular model.
Early or incompletely adapted type-1 bacteria, $X_t$, replicate, are lost, or convert irreversibly at rate $\nu$ into a later mammalian-adapted type-2 population, $Y_t$.
Either type may be associated with irreversible loss of intracellular containment at per-capita rates $\delta_1$ and $\delta_2$.
Containment failure terminates the single-cell process; subsequent extracellular growth lies outside the model.
The two states and rate assignments are coarse-grained modelling constructs rather than claims of a resolved molecular release mechanism.
