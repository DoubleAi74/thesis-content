# Figure 5.11 — caption

As typeset in `sections/08_conditional_means.tex`, label `dist:fig:threemeans`.
This file is a copy for reference; the chapter is the source of truth.

Three distinct averages at $(\lambda,\mu,\delta)=(1,0.2,0.05)$. The
unconditional mean release $V_\infty=13.99$ includes cells that never burst;
conditioning on eventual burst gives $V_\infty/(1-b)=a/(a-1)=17.23$;
conditioning on a very late eventual burst gives the size-biased limit
$(a+1)/(a-1)=33.46$. Keeping the conditioning explicit removes the apparent
circularity.
