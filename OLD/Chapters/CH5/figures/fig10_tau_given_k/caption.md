# Figure 5.10 — caption

As typeset in `sections/07_burst_time.tex`, label `dist:fig:taugivenk`.
This file is a copy for reference; the chapter is the source of truth.

Conditional mean rupture time given burst size $n$ when $\mu=0$,
with $\lambda=1$, $\delta=0.1$ and hence $\theta=1.1$. Exact harmonic means
$\theta^{-1}H_n$ from \cref{dist:eq:taugivenk} are shown with the large-$n$
approximation $\theta^{-1}(\log n+\gamma)$ and the linear budding comparator
$(n+1)/(d_{\Icell}+p)$ at $d_{\Icell}+p=8$. The two grow at different orders:
logarithmically for bursting, linearly for budding.
