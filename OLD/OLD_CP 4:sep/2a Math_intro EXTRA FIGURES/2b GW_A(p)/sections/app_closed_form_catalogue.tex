\section{Catalogue of exploratory formulae}
\label{a:app:catalogue}

This appendix records the remaining output of the closed-form searches described in
\cref{a:sec:GA}. The expressions below are approximations only.

\subsection*{The constrained rational candidate}

Polynomial and rational searches can be constrained to the known endpoints
$A(0)=\tfrac12$ and $A(\tfrac12)=0$. One of the more economical rational candidates
was $\hat A_1$ of \cref{a:eq:A1hat}, reproduced here for comparison:
\begin{equation*}
\hat A_1(p) = \frac{\tfrac12(1-2p)(1+2p)}
{1+\tfrac12p-\tfrac52p^2-\tfrac65p^3+\tfrac65p^4},
\qquad
\hat A_1'(\tfrac12) = -\frac{2}{0.55}\approx-3.636 .
\end{equation*}
It passes through both endpoints and misses the critical slope $-4$ required by
\cref{a:rem:gradient}.

\subsection*{Unconstrained symbolic tree search}

A less restricted symbolic tree search produced many longer formulae of similar
visual quality. Two representative outputs were
\begin{multline}
\label{a:eq:A2hat}
\hat A_2(p)
= 0.31696448624690005\,
\log\!\Biggl(
\Biggl|\cos\!\biggl(
\lrb{\frac{1}{3.19801133824532}}^{1/4}\\
{}+p\biggl[
\sin\!\Bigl(
\cos\!\bigl(|p|(0.621865696665606-p)\cos(\ee^{\ee^{p}})\bigr)
\Bigr)+|p|
\biggr]\biggr)\Biggr|
\Biggr)
+ 0.5982282661889831
\end{multline}
and $\hat A_3$ of \cref{a:eq:A3hat}. Both reproduce the target curve to within the
thickness of a plotted line across the whole interval (\cref{a:fig:A3hat}), and
neither is interpretable in any useful sense.

\subsection*{Spliced surrogates}

Forcing the correct critical branch by splicing gives $\hat A_4$ of
\cref{a:eq:A4hat}, which is a workable practical surrogate and nothing more.
