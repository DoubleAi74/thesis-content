**Figure | Competing catastrophe-rate regimes under an intracellular *Y. pestis* reading.**

(A) Survival $S(t)$ from one early founder.
(B) Conditional intensity $q_S(t)=-S'(t)/S(t)$.

Shared growth rates with $\delta_1+\delta_2=c=0.30$ as a coefficient normalisation (not matched realised exposure $\delta_1 X_t+\delta_2 Y_t$):
EQ $(0.15,0.15)$, MAT $(0.05,0.25)$, GATE $(0,0.30)$, EARLY $(0.25,0.05)$
($\lambda_1=1$, $\mu_1=0.45$, $\nu=0.35$, $\lambda_2=0.85$, $\mu_2=0.40$).

Parameters are illustrative, not fitted. MAT is a working hypothesis; GATE is the type-2-only extreme ($S'(0)=0$). Under this normalisation $S$ separates only modestly, while early $q_S$ separates sharply because $q_S(0)=\delta_1$.

Exact closed form for $S$; $q_S$ from the ODE right-hand side on the solution path.
