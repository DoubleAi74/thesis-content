Contributions at a glance

Bursting versus budding                                                                                Ch. 6, 8
At matched mean output, bursting lowers extinction risk in the supercritical regime under linear
clearance exactly when L > 1; offspring laws coincide at L = 1. With a positive integer removal budget
on one delivery, bursting leaves more surviving output exactly when L < 1.

An exact two-type solution                                                                               Ch. 7
Closed forms for the no-catastrophe probability and joint generating function, with one-way conversion
between types and type-specific catastrophe rates.

The statistics of one bursting cell                                                                   Chs. 4, 5
A unified calculation of load and release moments, burst timing and the burst-size/quasi-stationary
identity, extended to several founders and successive cell-to-cell transfers.

From cells to infection dynamics                                                                         Ch. 6
An age-structured model derived from single-cell laws. Fitted rates depend on the growth phase; one
exponential phase cannot identify all three intracellular rates.

Kolmogorov’s constant and the logistic map                                                               Ch. 3
Identifies A(p) through the Koenigs function and proves that function hypertranscendental in its spatial
argument. The closed-form question for A(p) remains open.

Evolution under changing rates                                                                           Ch. 9
A limiting species-count law and variance under capacity-dependent speciation, and an explicit moving
critical point for competitive reversal in the Pimentel model.


Original statements, extensions and their published foundations are distinguished in Originality of the results,
p. 269.
