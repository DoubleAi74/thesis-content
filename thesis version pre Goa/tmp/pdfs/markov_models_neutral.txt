Scope and conventions

This document lists every stochastic model in the source thesis whose law is specified: a state
space plus a transition mechanism (transition matrix, generator, jump rates, or transition kernel).
Deterministic population equations are included only where the thesis derives them as exact
projections of a stochastic model, and the entry says so. Derived results are quoted only where
they are part of the model’s working apparatus (characteristic roots, regime boundaries, working
parameter points).
   Time is continuous and chains are time homogeneous unless an entry says otherwise; o(∆t)
terms are suppressed. Per-capita rates multiply the relevant count to give total rates. Model
numbers M1–M31 are used for cross-reference. Sections are cited by thesis chapter and section
number only.

2.6     M6: The birth–death–catastrophe (BDC) process
Specified in Definition in §2.2.2.5; restated in §4.2; canonical restatement and notation in §5.2.1–5.2.2.
Conventions: §4.2.3–4.2.4. Variant: several founders, §5.9..


State space. {0, 1, 2, . . .} ∪ {H}: the load within a compartment while it remains intact, with
H a distinct absorbing rupture state. Both 0 and H are absorbing.

2.7      M7: Seasonal two-population process
Specified in §2.2.3.1.


State space.       {0, 1, . . .}2 : counts Xt , Yt of two interacting populations.

Transitions.       Time-inhomogeneous generator

      q(x,y),(x+1,y) (t) = bS + (t) x,       q(x,y),(x−1,y+1) (t) = exy,          q(x,y),(x,y−1) (t) = dy,       (2.14)

with seasonal multiplier S + (t) = max{0, 1 + A sin(ωt)} (clipped because a rate cannot be
negative). For A > 1 each period 2π/ω contains a closed season of length (2/ω) arccos(1/A).
On the axis y = 0 the X population is a time-inhomogeneous Yule process; in the interior
the first moments involve E(Xt Yt ) and do not close. The deterministic comparison system is
ẋ = bS + (t)x − exy, ẏ = exy − dy. Exact paths are simulated by thinning against S + (t) ≤ 1 + A.
