\section{Summary of results}
\label{bdc:sec:summary}

For reference, \cref{bdc:tab:notation} collects the notation,
\cref{bdc:tab:closedforms} the principal closed forms, and
\cref{bdc:tab:odes} the four differential equations from which they are
obtained. Together with $I(0)=J(0)=K(0)=1$ and $V(0)=0$, these equations are
enough to reconstruct the moment calculation.

The roots also give a compact view of parameter dependence.
\Cref{bdc:fig:landscape} shows $b$, the probability of internal extinction
before rupture, and $V_\infty$, the mean release over all realisations, across
the $(\mu,\delta)$ plane with $\lambda$ fixed. Increasing the death rate makes
internal extinction more likely; increasing the catastrophe rate makes it less
likely. Mean release is largest when catastrophe is rare enough to permit
growth, although internal death continues to compete with that growth.
